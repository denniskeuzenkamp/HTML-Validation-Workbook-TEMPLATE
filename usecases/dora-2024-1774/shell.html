Source register

Eligibility is proposed automatically from the document name and content and must be confirmed by the auditor. TD = Test of Design, DE = Design Effectiveness, OE = Operating Effectiveness. Only eligible documents can support the corresponding conclusion. Relevance, linked requirement and linked class are filled by the Matching Engine and are candidate links, not proof.

Validation sheet

Workflow: (1) Normbuilder Engine defines the four requirement classes per requirement; (2) Matching Engine embeds evidence chunks and matches them to the requirement classes (not only the raw requirement text); (3) AI validation assesses TD, DE and OE separately. Embedding similarity is a candidate match and never proof of compliance. Design-only evidence cannot support an OE conclusion; operating evidence cannot support TD or DE unless it also describes the control design. Missing evidence is not compliance. Automated results are indicative – the auditor must confirm or override each conclusion (IIA Standards 14.2–14.3, 14.6).
